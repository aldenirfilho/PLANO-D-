# Arte dos cinco cavaleiros do PLANO D

Solicitação: nova imagem para o site, com cinco personagens cósmicos, fogo, auras de energia e uma missão de fé, solidariedade, cuidado socioambiental e inclusão.

Ferramenta: geração de imagens integrada (image_gen), em 20/10/2026.

Original preservado: `dist/assets/cavaleiros-plano-d.png` (1536 × 1024). A versão WebP da página usa a mesma composição, sem recorte. Nenhum dado privado do BOLSA AJUDA foi enviado no prompt.

## Prompt final

```text
Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: final cinematic hero artwork for the Brazilian community website PLANO D.
Create exactly FIVE distinct cosmic riders / astronaut guardians of a fictional apocalypse of renewal. The user requests a Ghost Rider-like flaming space astronaut with a Super Saiyan-like blazing energy aura, expanded into a five-person heroic team. Original character designs, no movie or anime logos.
All five must be clearly visible and countable, each with an armored astronaut body and a flaming skull-like luminous face inside an open or translucent space helmet. Central rider has blazing orange and gold fire, black metallic armored spacesuit and a hovering cosmic motorcycle; the other four flank them in a shallow V, each beside or astride a futuristic motorcycle with fiery wheels. Distinct auras: cyan electricity, emerald living energy, violet plasma, and white-gold fire. Their silhouettes feel rebellious, powerful, aerospace and mythical, facing the viewer, protective and united, no attacking or weapons.
Story context: they came to Earth to protect humanity from greed and indifference, inspire voluntary giving to social and environmental organizations, rekindle faith in God, restore human dignity and champion neurodivergent inclusion and collaboration. Convey this through visual symbolism: small verdant growth emerging from cracked ground, radiant Earth on horizon, a subtle cross-shaped star above the team. No literal money, no labels or text.
Composition: impressive premium dark sci-fi comic cover / richly painted cinematic concept art, landscape 3:2, five figures fully distinguishable across the frame, sufficient breathing room around helmets and fire. Earth and atmospheric space backdrop, midnight navy, cyan rim light and vivid amber flame. Fire should be dramatic and beautiful, detailed flame tongues and energetic ascending halos, not gore or horror. Strong readable silhouettes, intricate realistic armor, layered depth, cinematic light, polished professional quality.
Constraints: exactly five guardians, no crowds, no extra faces, no text, no lettering, no watermarks, no logos, no gore.
```

## Identificação na página

Da esquerda para a direita: Nexo (ciano, colaboração), Raiz (verde, natureza), Brasa (laranja, solidariedade), Prisma (violeta, neurodiversidade) e Lúmen (dourado, fé em Deus).

A narrativa é ficcional. Os personagens não são apresentados como personagens bíblicos, como interpretação literal do Apocalipse ou como afirmações clínicas sobre neurodivergência.
